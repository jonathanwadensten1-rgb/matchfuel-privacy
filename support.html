Restoring your Pro subscription

Open Matchfuel → Settings, tap "Upgrade to Matchfuel Pro", then Restore Purchases. This re-syncs your subscription from the Apple ID you purchased with.

Managing or canceling your subscription

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Matchfuel.

Deleting your data

All of your data is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
