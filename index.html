Matchfuel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Matchfuel is built to work entirely on your device. This page explains, plainly, what that means.

Data we collect

None of your data is sent to us or to any third party. Your profile, meal log, schedule, custom recipes, and settings are stored only in a local database on your device. We have no server, no account system, and no way to see your data.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you grant permission, Matchfuel reads your sleep and Apple Watch active-energy data from Apple Health to personalize fueling suggestions — for example, adjusting a plan after a poor night's sleep. This access is read-only, the data is processed entirely on your device, and it is never transmitted anywhere or shared with us.

Subscriptions & payments

Matchfuel Pro purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles that information.

Notifications

Meal reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent from a server, and we have no visibility into when or whether you receive them.

Exporting your data

Matchfuel includes a backup/export feature that lets you export your own data using your device's native share sheet. This is entirely user-initiated — the data goes wherever you choose to send it, and only if you choose to.

No accounts, no analytics, no ads

Matchfuel doesn't require an account or login. It does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its locally stored data from your device. Since nothing is stored elsewhere, there is nothing further to delete on our end.

Children

Matchfuel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them — in practice, this is straightforward, since the app doesn't collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
